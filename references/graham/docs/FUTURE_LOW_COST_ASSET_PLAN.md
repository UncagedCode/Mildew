# Future Low-Cost Graham Asset Plan

This document exists to stop the project from spending generation budget indiscriminately.

## Free / already-paid improvements first

Before generating anything new:

1. Extract alternate frames from existing videos.
2. Re-edit timing and camera cuts.
3. Reuse reading-cards and reaction shots.
4. Continue voice over non-Graham graphics.
5. Use the existing short motion excerpts.
6. Create new framing/crops from existing 720p masters.
7. Add sound design, audience reactions and silence.

## Very low-cost future image tasks

When image-generation access is available, prioritize **still edits**, not video:

1. Clean blink pair from canonical neutral.
2. Front-facing disappointed state.
3. Front-facing mild irritation.
4. Opposite-direction look.
5. Rattled state.
6. Controlled angry state.
7. Off-air exhausted state.

Each new image should preserve:

- exact face/age/hair;
- same suit/shirt/tie unless a story reason exists;
- cue-card dimensions;
- camera/lens perspective;
- set lighting;
- 4:3 broadcast framing.

## Paid video only when motion itself matters

Good reasons for future video generation:

- Graham physically loses his temper;
- apparent off-air commercial-break performance;
- major opening/entrance;
- rare Broadcast Incident;
- complex interaction with crew/prop.

Bad reasons:

- reading a scoreboard;
- saying a player's name;
- explaining ordinary rules;
- introducing a standard question;
- smiling or frowning briefly.

Those should be solved by editing/stills/audio.

## Budget philosophy

Every future paid generation should have a filename and gameplay purpose **before** it is generated.

Example good request:

`graham_angry_camera1_studio_c_interruption_08s`

Not:

`make more Graham videos`
