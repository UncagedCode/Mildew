## Character identity

Graham Mildew should read as a completely plausible late-1990s British regional / low-budget game-show host.

### Approximate appearance

- Male, roughly late 40s to mid-50s.
- Clean-shaven.
- Receding / thinning, neatly side-parted light-brown-to-dark-blond hair.
- Ordinary, recognisably human face; not a caricature and not overtly creepy.
- Heavy but plausible television makeup.
- Slightly tired eyes beneath the presenter's performance.
- Dark, cheap-but-presentable suit.
- Pale shirt.
- Loud abstract-pattern 1990s tie.
- Small lapel microphone.
- Cue cards carrying MILDEW branding.
- Upright, trained presenter posture.

### The important contradiction

Graham is visually ordinary. The unsettling quality comes from **brief failures of the television persona**, not monster design.

Default Graham:

- warm;
- practiced;
- professional;
- slightly cheesy;
- confident;
- very comfortable on camera.

Under pressure:

- jaw tightens;
- smile fades rather than snapping off;
- eyes move before the head;
- he may look toward crew or backstage;
- irritation is underplayed;
- when he returns to the camera, the presenter mask rebuilds gradually.

The final clip's off-camera reaction and recovery are the key performance reference.

## Canonical expression hierarchy

### Neutral
`assets/stills/canonical/neutral_front.png`

Use for:

- waiting;
- very short silent holds;
- deadpan response;
- pre-cue moments;
- intentionally uncomfortable staring.

### Restrained presenter smile
`assets/stills/canonical/restrained_smile.png`

This should be the **default friendly expression**. It is preferable to the broader smile for routine hosting.

### Presenter smile
`assets/stills/canonical/presenter_smile.png`

Use for:

- opening applause;
- large reveals;
- congratulating players;
- deliberate game-show enthusiasm.

Do not keep Graham in this expression constantly.

### Presenting / speaking
`assets/stills/canonical/presenting_speaking.png`

Use as a short visible speech state, but do not attempt to leave this frame on screen for long dynamic dialogue. Cut to graphics while the voice continues.

### Irritated turn
`assets/stills/canonical/irritated_turn.png`

Beginning of an off-camera reaction.

### Irritated hold
`assets/stills/canonical/irritated_hold.png`

Strongest existing annoyed state. This should remain rare enough to matter.

### Return tense
`assets/stills/canonical/return_tense.png`

Graham has returned toward the audience/camera but has not fully restored the television persona.

### Recovered presenter
`assets/stills/canonical/recovered_presenter.png`

A restrained recovery state after irritation. Prefer this over jumping directly to the broad smile.

### Reading cue cards
`assets/stills/canonical/reading_cards.png`

Extremely useful state. It gives the broadcast somewhere natural to put Graham while:

- dynamic speech is being assembled;
- a scoreboard is about to appear;
- the Director needs a beat;
- an audience reaction plays;
- an irregularity is about to happen.

## Studio status

The generated studio is a valuable **tone and composition reference**, but it is not immutable production canon.

Preserve the characteristics:

- cheap MDF set construction;
- curved green panels;
- chunky MILDEW lettering;
- patterned carpet;
- coloured studio lights;
- silver/chrome trim;
- warm late-90s game-show lighting;
- visible artificiality;
- real-TV rather than modern analogue-horror aesthetic.

The main Mildew art bible may refine layout, podiums, cameras and backstage geography.

## Broadcaster branding

The source generation included a BBC-style on-screen bug by accident. This is explicitly non-canon. Curated assets have had it removed. Do not infer that Mildew aired on the BBC or any other real broadcaster.
